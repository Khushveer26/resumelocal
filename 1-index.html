How this score works (Transparency & Formula Breakdown)
Job Keyword / Skill Category Weight Status Evaluation Details

Boundary-Aware Matching: RegEx boundaries prevent false positives (e.g., "Java" will not match "JavaScript", and "C" will not match arbitrary words containing the letter C).

Special Tokens: Symbols in programming terms such as C++, C#, .NET, and Node.js are preserved with punctuation-safe delimiters.

Alias Deduplication: Standard industry aliases (e.g., JS → JavaScript, React.js → React, Postgres → PostgreSQL) are mapped to canonical forms so they count exactly once.

No Artificial Inflation: Repetition of a keyword in either document does not increase its weight or score.

Explicit Limitations: Negation (e.g., "no Python required"), depth of experience (e.g., "5 years of Java" vs "introductory Java"), and contextual semantics beyond explicit aliases are not reliably understood.

Prototype Disclaimer: This is a keyword-based college project prototype, not an official ATS evaluation or a guarantee of selection.

Analysis runs in your browser. PDF upload loads a third-party parsing library; your resume is not sent for analysis.
